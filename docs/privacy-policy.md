# Commonplace privacy policy

*Last updated: [DATE]. Commonplace is published by [PUBLISHER NAME], [COUNTRY]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].*

Commonplace turns the reels and posts you save into notes. This page explains what that involves.

## What stays on your phone

Your notes, your own notes on them, the videos and images you add (until the note is made, unless you choose to keep them), and your settings. None of this leaves your phone except as described below.

## What is sent, and to whom

**To make a note**, the video or images you add are sent through the Commonplace server to Google's Gemini AI. Gemini reads what is said and written in them and writes the note. The server deletes the files from Gemini as soon as they are read. They are never stored on the Commonplace server.

Commonplace uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms, Google may use content sent to it to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Don't add videos or images with anything private in them. See [Google's Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

**When you sign in with Google**, Commonplace receives your Google account's email address, name and account ID. The server keeps these to know which notes count against your daily limit, along with how many notes you made each day.

**When you buy Commonplace Plus**, Google Play handles the payment. Commonplace receives a purchase token from Google Play, and the server checks it with Google Play to know you have Plus. Commonplace never sees your payment details.

Everything is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Commonplace does not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**. This deletes your account and everything the server keeps about you right away. The notes on your phone stay until you delete them or uninstall the app. Deleting the account does not cancel a Plus subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

If you can no longer use the app, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

## Age

Commonplace is for people 18 and older.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
