Your saved reels, as notes you'll actually read.

Commonplace is a free Android app that turns the Instagram reels you save into notes: the key points, things to try, and everything that was said. It runs on your own free Gemini API key.

From saved to noted, on its own

You hear something useful, tap save, and tell yourself you'll watch it later. Later never comes. Commonplace makes the note while the reel is still fresh.

  1. Save or share a reel

    Save it on Instagram as usual and it shows up in the app on its own. Or tap Share, then Commonplace.

  2. Gemini watches it

    It hears what's said and reads the text on screen. The video is made smaller before it's sent and deleted after.

  3. Read the note

    A plain title, a short summary, key points with the important phrase highlighted, and things to try.

One older note a day

The home screen brings back something you saved before, so saves don't disappear.

In your language

Pick the language notes are written in. Rewrite any note in another one without sending the video again.

Copy for AI chats

One tap copies a note with everything that was said. Copy all takes every note at once, ready to paste into any AI chat.

A queue you control

Tap Start now on a waiting reel to make it first, or Cancel on one that's stuck. One reel never holds up the rest.

No account, no server

Notes live on your phone. The app talks only to Google for the note and Instagram for your saves.

Free, with your own Gemini key

Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey: sign in with Google, tap Create API key, paste it into the app. Don't turn on billing, and it stays free. The free daily limit is plenty for a few reels a day.

Every note shows exactly what it used. On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products, so don't send anything private.

What Gemini used

Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite: 3,376 tokens in, 525 out. €0.0021 at Google's paid price. On a free-tier key (no billing) you pay nothing.

Questions

Does it need my Instagram password?

No. You sign in on Instagram's own page inside the app. Commonplace keeps only the session, encrypted on the phone and left out of backups. Signing in is optional: sharing works without it.

Is reading my Saved list allowed?

Instagram has no official API for saved posts, so Commonplace reads them the way instagram.com does, with your own sign-in: only your own saves, only while the app is open, and it stops at any security check. Instagram's terms don't allow automated access, so if that ever stops working, sharing a downloaded video still does.

How do I install it?

On your phone, tap Download for Android. When the download finishes, open Commonplace.apk and tap Install. The first time, Android asks to allow your browser to install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install. If Android says the app isn't compatible, get the version for all phones.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Commonplace is built in Flutter and almost all of it is shared, so iOS is possible later.

Can I see the code?

All of it, on GitHub, under the MIT license. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
